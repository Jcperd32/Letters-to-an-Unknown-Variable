Think Pieces

I am going to be honest here. Sometimes I am not entirely sure if I am living real life or stuck in a nightmare. This world has been formed the way it is for centuries, and unfortunately, most of it is driven by greed. It is sad to think about because we are all human beings, and yet, depending on where you are born, it sort of limits the future you might have. Now the truth is that some are even denied that gift of ever seeing a future.

There are issues in Sudan, the Congo, Palestine, and so many more. It is heartbreaking that we, as people and human beings with emotions that we all share, are so willing to look the other way as long as it does not affect us. We say we care about others, but I have yet to see that reflected much. Even from me. I am no different than the rest, I think. I am complicit in some way, and all I can do is try my best to help any way I can. But it does not change the fact that I could do more.

How many people need to die? How many people need to be displaced or separated from their family for the people in charge to feel a sense of remorse? Living real life truly does show you that, besides the fact that not everyone will think the same way you do, there is the reality that some are so willing to be horrendous human beings with no remorse for anything. I am not sure I could ever imagine that such a type of character was possible.

But we need to be our own people who fight for what is right. To show a bit of change, even if it is minimum. Even if we think it is nothing. Just a bit to show our humanity. To give back to this earth that we should all truly love, and learn to feel for others. Because when it is our turn to be in their position, they will probably do as we did. Or perhaps they will all inevitably be long gone, and that is why we will be next.
