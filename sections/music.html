Music and their power

This is where it gets interesting, I suppose. I cannot claim to have a defined taste in music. It is more a collection of fragments, pieces gathered over time from everywhere and nowhere. But I want to share a few that have lingered.

There is something fascinating about music itself, how it behaves. It is a language of its own, built on patterns and rhythms that feel almost innate, like a code we are born understanding. But that is not why we listen, is it?

It is no secret that my favorite artist is Taylor Swift. I can already feel the eye rolling from here. But it was not always that way. For the longest time, I did not truly hear her, aside from the hits everyone knows. My main artist was Selena Gomez. Then came someone who wanted to change that. And I suppose they did.

All it took was one dedicated song. It is a disarming feeling, having a song given to you. You wonder what they are trying to tell you with these lyrics, this melody. Why this artist, and not any other?

It intrigued me. So I did what anyone does when they are curious: I listened more. I wanted to understand what they heard in it. We all want people to experience the things we love the same way we do. It is only human.

But that is the magic of music. It evokes emotions and memories, sometimes because of the words, sometimes in spite of them. I believe we can assign a unique song to every person we encounter. A theme, a feeling, a few minutes of sound that somehow captures their essence.

I do that. And if you have made it this far, I would be happy to share what yours might be. All you have to do is ask.

The songs here, then, are not just songs. They are markers. They represent points in my life, and the people who walked through them. Some who still do, and some whose footsteps have long since faded.
