Music and their power

We've reached one of my favorite parts - sharing music. This is where I get real, because music means too much to me to fake it. Some people don't get that? Fine. But here's the thing: I'm about to say stuff most don't know about me. If you care, great. If not? Well, it's my page anyway.

Full confession: My favorite artist is Taylor Swift (shocking). Except that's not the whole truth. Before 2020, I didn't care about her - Selena Gomez was my person. But Taylor...she became something else. A figment keeping memories alive. Reminiscence? Maybe.

That was when I realized music's power - or rather, when you made me realize it. You with your stupid positive worldview, always seeing good everywhere, always wanting to do more. Cleverness I can process - but that relentless kindness? Still haven't cracked that code

You taught me to actually listen - to lyrics, to moments songs drag back up. Some are straight poetry (yes, even WAP - future English classes will study those metaphors, mark my words).
